FAQ
Common Questions
Is this site affiliated with any agent?

No. Rep Fashion Cult is completely independent. Agents listed are simply options for buyers — we have no financial relationship with any of them.

How do you verify items?

Community members buy items and submit QC photos. We cross-reference multiple sources and buyers before adding items to the spreadsheet.

Are reps legal to buy?

Laws vary by country. In most places, buying replica goods for personal use is a grey area. Selling them commercially is typically illegal. Know your local laws.

How do I submit a QC photo?

Click the "Submit QC" button on the QC page and upload your photos. Our team reviews submissions within 24-48 hours before publishing.
